WORD OF GOD — KJV BIBLE

This is a small installable website (PWA).

To test locally:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open a terminal in the extracted folder.
3. Run: py -m http.server 8000
4. Visit http://localhost:8000

To publish:
1. Upload the extracted folder contents to a static web host such as Netlify.
2. Open the published HTTPS URL on your iPhone in Safari.
3. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen.

The Bible text is fetched online from bible-api.com (KJV). Internet is needed to load Scripture.
Speech uses the device/browser's built-in text-to-speech.
